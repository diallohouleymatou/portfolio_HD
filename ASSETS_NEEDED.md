# Required Assets

To make your portfolio feel "alive" and professional, please provide the following assets.
You can drag and drop these files into the corresponding folders in `public/assets/`.

## 1. Personal Branding
**Path:** `public/assets/images/`
- [ ] `headshot.jpg` (Professional photo for About section)
- [ ] `logo.svg` or `logo.png` (Your personal logo, if you have one. Otherwise we use the text logo)

## 2. Project Screenshots (Crucial!)
**Path:** `public/assets/images/projects/`
For each project, please provide at least one high-quality screenshot or mockup.

- [ ] `gnawalma-cover.jpg` (The tailor shop app UI)
- [ ] `koala-cover.jpg` (Finance app mobile screen)
- [ ] `lic-cover.jpg` (LIC website)
