## Recruiter Interpretation Risks

Strong recruiters / strong hiring managers will infer:
- You are already an engineer and consultant, not an AI beginner.
- Certifications are supplementary, not your core proof.
- You are transitioning in a disciplined way toward applied AI systems.

Weak recruiters may misread:
- "Certification collector" profile
- Inflated AI title without delivery proof
- Random low-level cert with no role fit

Compensation strategy in wording:
- Always pair AI claims with business-system delivery bullets.
- Use verbs like `integrated`, `operationalized`, `instrumented`, `translated requirements`, `automated workflows`.
- Avoid claims like `AI expert`, `AI architect`, `LLM specialist` unless proven by shipped projects.

## Bullet Framing Pattern for Experience
Use this pattern repeatedly:
- Business context + system scope + technical action + measurable operational effect.

Example:
"Led migration and redesign of ERP workflows across logistics, purchasing, and finance domains; implemented controls and reporting layers improving operational traceability across 6 warehouses and 30 routes."
